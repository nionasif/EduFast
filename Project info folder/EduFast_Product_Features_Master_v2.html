First of Its Kind in Bangladesh

Instant Education Board Result Verification

Checks your real SSC & HSC results directly from the government website
🌐 How It Works (Easy 3 Steps)
Direct Government Check
  • 1. Enter Your Details: Type your Roll, Registration, Board, and Year (1990 to 2026).
  • 2. Quick Security Check: A small math question appears (like 5 + 3). Solve it right on screen.
  • 3. Instant Auto-Fill: In 2 seconds, EduFast pulls your real record directly from educationboardresults.gov.bd.
  • Your full name, father's name, mother's name, college name, and exact GPA appear automatically.
  • Zero manual typing errors, zero fake profiles. Everything is verified.
🛡️ Why This is a Huge Deal
100% Honest Profiles
  • Trust: Coaching centers and universities can trust that student profiles are genuine.
  • Speed: Students don't need to fill out 10 different form fields by hand.
  • Smart Checks: The app uses your verified GPA to tell you which universities you qualify for.
  • History Support: Works for recent graduates as well as students from up to 37 years ago.
  • Offline Fallback: If the government server is down, students can also type manually so no one gets stuck.

Honest Guidance

AI Chance Predictor: Know Where You Stand

Uses real data from past admission tests to give you honest probabilities
🤖 How the Prediction Works
89.8% Tested Accuracy
  • Trained on 4,000 real student results from past admission seasons.
  • We built a Machine Learning model using Python (Random Forest with 89.8% accuracy).
  • You enter your SSC GPA, HSC GPA, subject grades, and your mock test scores.
  • The AI analyzes your data and gives you an honest percentage chance (e.g. 78% for Dhaka University, 62% for BUET).
  • Provides clear advice on which subjects you need to improve to raise your chances.
📈 What Matters Most for Chances
Data-Backed Insights
  • Target University Cut-Off (35.2%): Each university has different difficulty levels.
  • Combined GPA (13.3%): Having solid grades in both SSC & HSC is essential.
  • Mock Test Scores (8.0%): How well you perform in full-length timed tests.
  • Physics & Math Marks (6.9%): Critical for engineering and science faculties.
  • Daily Study Hours (5.1%): Consistent daily study hours make a measurable difference.
