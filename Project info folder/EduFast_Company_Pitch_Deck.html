🌐 Revolutionary Breakthrough

Direct Government Education Board Verification

The first Bangladeshi EdTech platform with live WAF/CAPTCHA bypassing board scrapers

EduFast reverse-engineers the Ministry of Education results portal (educationboardresults.gov.bd/v2/home), enabling instant, automated verification of official student credentials.

How It Operates:
  1. Student enters Roll, Registration, Board, and Year (1990–2026 history supported).
  2. Backend initializes a secure session token and fetches the official server CAPTCHA.
  3. Student verifies CAPTCHA in an interactive modal.
  4. System extracts genuine GPA, Student Name, Parents' Names, and School/College.
